Help and FAQ

Quick answers to the questions we hear most. Can't find yours? Contact us.

Booking and availability

How do I book a vehicle?
  1. Open Vehicles, pick a vehicle and choose your pickup and return days on the calendar.
  2. Fill in the rental details (name, NIC and, for self drive, your driving licence ID).
  3. Check the summary, then pay by card, wallet, bank transfer or cash.

You need a free account to book. It takes a minute to create one.

How long is a vehicle held while I pay?

Once you confirm the summary the vehicle is held for you for 15 minutes. A countdown on the page shows the time left. If it runs out the hold is released and you can book again.

The calendar shows some days crossed out. Why?

Crossed-out days are already booked by someone else. Pick a free stretch of days and the price appears straight away. A vehicle that is out on rental can still be booked for dates after it comes back.

What is the longest rental, and who can rent?

A single booking can last up to 60 days. Renters must be between 18 and 80 years old.

Can I cancel a booking?

Yes. Open My bookings and choose Cancel on any booking that is not finished yet. The vehicle is released straight away. If you had already paid the rental, the payment is refunded. A bank transfer that is still being checked is simply closed.

Drivers and locations

Self drive or with a driver: what is the difference?

With self drive you drive the vehicle and show your driving licence at pickup. With a driver a professional driver is assigned for the whole trip, so you don't need to show a licence. The driver fee is LKR 3500 per day and is added to the price before you pay.

After payment we assign a driver and send them the trip details. Bikes are self drive only.

Can I pick up and drop off at different places?

Yes. Choose any of our branches for pickup and for drop-off. Returning to a different branch adds a one-way fee of LKR 2500. With a driver you can also give an address and the driver comes to you.

Documents

What do I need to bring when I collect the vehicle?

Bring your original NIC and, for self drive, your original driving licence. Our staff check them before the keys are handed over. Copies and photos are not accepted.

Why do you ask for my NIC when I book?

Renter details are needed to prepare the rental and to check your identity at pickup. We read your age and sex from the NIC number so you have less to type, and we remember your details for your next booking.

Prices and payments

Which payment methods can I use?
  • Card and digital wallet: confirmed straight away.
  • Bank transfer: send the money, enter your transfer details, and our team confirms it. The booking shows Payment under review until then.
  • Cash: pay at the counter and our staff record it.
Are there any discounts?
  • 5% off rentals of 7 days or more.
  • 2% off when your rental starts on a Saturday.
  • Premium members get 10% off rentals of 3 days or more, and a gentler late-return fee.

Discounts are applied automatically and listed line by line in your price before you pay.

What happens if I return the vehicle late?

Late returns are charged 10% per day. The booking then shows Late fine due and you pay the fine online from the booking page. Return on time and there is nothing extra to pay.

Where can I get my receipt?

Every payment gets a receipt. Open the booking or Payment history to view it on screen or download it as a PDF. We also email it to you.

Your account

I did not get my verification code

The code arrives by email and works for 15 minutes. Check your spam folder, then use Resend code after a minute to get a new one.

How do reviews work?

After a rental is completed you can rate it from 1 to 5 stars and leave a comment. Honest reviews are published, including low ratings.

Something went wrong. How do I complain?

Open Complaints, choose New complaint and tell us what happened. You can follow our replies and the status on the same page.
